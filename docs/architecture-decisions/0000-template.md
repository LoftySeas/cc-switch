# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** Name or team

- **Related:** Links to vision, architecture, milestone, issue, or prior ADR
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Superseded by:** None

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, forces, and evidence. State why a durable
decision is needed now. Separate facts from assumptions.

## Decision drivers

- Driver or invariant
- Security, privacy, reliability, operability, or compatibility constraint
- Cost or delivery constraint

## Considered options

### Option A: Name

Describe the option, strengths, weaknesses, risks, and operational implications.

### Option B: Name

Describe the option, strengths, weaknesses, risks, and operational implications.

## Decision

State the selected option and the binding rules it introduces. Be specific enough
that a reviewer can determine whether an implementation complies.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expected benefit

### Negative

- Accepted cost or limitation

### Risks and mitigations

- Risk and corresponding mitigation or detection method

## Validation

Describe how the decision will be tested, measured, or reviewed and who owns that
validation.
